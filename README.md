# NoHallu

---
## 📌 Overview

**NoHallu** is a research-driven project that tackles the challenge of **AI hallucinations** in multimodal systems. Developed during the **SoNIC Research Workshop** at Cornell University (Summer 2024), NoHallu explores how to combine large vision-language models like **LLaVA** with domain-specific classifiers like **BioCLIP** to verify and ground AI outputs in citizen science contexts.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

To address the problem of AI hallucinations by integrating multimodal models and applying targeted problem engineering strategies. NoHallu focuses specifically on the **citizen science domain**, aiming to:

- Combine general-purpose vision-language models (e.g., LLaVA) with domain-specific tools (e.g., BioCLIP)
- Improve the factual grounding and trustworthiness of AI outputs in real-world, user-generated scientific observations
